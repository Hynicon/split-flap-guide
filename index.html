Split-flap build guide

From printed parts and bare PCBs to a working display. It follows David Königsmann's original instructions (Printables 69464) and his photos, changed for this build: shortened case, hand-soldered boards, and an ESP32 in place of the ESP-01. Hole positions and screw paths come from the CAD model. Pick your size at the top and every quantity updates. Ticks are saved in this browser, and Next step in the top bar jumps to the first step you haven't ticked. Steps with a 3D button show that step in the stage's 3D diagram. Click any photo to enlarge it. The firmware, the phone app and the remote server are in the split-flap repository.

4 · First unit test

one unit, before building the rest

On the PC, in a terminal opened in a copy of the split-flap repository (the folder with platformio.ini), with the Nano on USB and the unit on 12 V through the power lead in U8. Stop pio device monitor with Ctrl+C before each upload, because it holds the port.

  1. If the upload fails with a sync error, the Nano has the newer bootloader: change board to nanoatmega328new in the unit and offset_tool sections of platformio.ini.
  2. If a neighbouring flap shows, repeat step 1 with a larger or smaller offset. About 45 steps is one flap.
ThenBuild the remaining 8 drums and units, and run steps 1–2 on each Nano with its own DIP setting.

5 · Case

files for 9 units

Read this first

  • Front is the side where the flaps show. Back is the side the PCBs face. Left and right are always as seen from the front, so when you work from the back, the left end is on your right.
  • The case is three layers, front to back: front cover (an open box), middle frame (a flat 15 mm plate with one window per unit) and back cover (a shallow tray). They sit edge to edge, held by screws; nothing slides inside anything.
  • Each layer is printed as Left, Center and Right pieces. At each seam, the Center piece's tabs lie over the ends of the Left or Right piece, in the top and bottom edges. A joint screw goes in from the outside top or bottom edge, through the Center tab, into a nut sitting in a hex pocket inside the Left or Right piece. The pocket opens on the inside of the case.
  • Work on a soft cloth. Melt inserts at about 200 °C, push straight, and stop when they're flush.

6 · Power and ESP32

Option B in hardware/wiring.md
3.3 V onlyThe ESP32 is not 5 V tolerant. Never connect SDA/SCL straight to the bus. Set the buck converter to 5.0 V before connecting anything to it.

Troubleshooting

hardware faults, by symptom
SymptomCheck
No 5 V at the Nano header, or the 7805 gets hotDisconnect the power. Check the 7805's direction (stage 1, step 5), then repeat the meter check for shorts (step 6). Look for solder bridges on U4.
Upload to a Nano fails with a sync errorThe Nano has the newer bootloader: set board to nanoatmega328new (stage 4, step 1). If the port is busy, stop pio device monitor first.
The sensor's LED never lights when the magnet is nearThe hall cable is wired by position and not by function, so the sensor has no power (stage 2, step 2). Check that the board has 12 V.
A neighbouring flap shows after homingRun the offset tool again for that unit (stage 4, step 1). One flap is about 45 steps.
A drum keeps turning and never homesThe sensor doesn't see the magnet. Check that the magnet's marked face points out (stage 2, step 4), that it passes close to the sensor (stage 3, step 6), and that the sensor is plugged into U6. A unit gives up after 3 turns. See also step 4 of the bring-up list in hardware/wiring.md.
The scan shows one unit as MISSINGCheck its DIP switch against the table in stage 7, then power it off and on, because the switch is only read at power-up. Check its bus cable (stage 5, step 6) and that its Nano is fully seated. The Nano must run this repo's unit firmware.
No unit answers the scanSDA and SCL are swapped, a GND is missing, or the level shifter is wired wrong. Compare every wire with the drawing in stage 6.
Units stutter or restart when they all moveThe 12 V rail is sagging: each 7805 needs at least 7 V in. Use a 12 V supply of 3 A or more, fit the 1000 µF capacitor, and keep the power wires short (stage 7, step 2).
The web page is blank or shows 404The filesystem wasn't uploaded: pio run -e master -t uploadfs (stage 6, step 4).
Wi-Fi, accounts, tokens or wrong countsSee Troubleshooting in the README.
